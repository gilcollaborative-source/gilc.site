Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide to us directly when submitting an inquiry or booking an advisory session, including your full name, email address, telephone/WeChat contact details, and academic interests.

2. Use of Information

Your information is used solely to facilitate educational advising, coordinate faculty mentorship matching, deliver service receipts, and respond to your inquiries. We do not sell or lease your personal information to third parties.

3. Payment Processing Security

Payment information (such as credit card details, WeChat Pay, or Alipay credentials) is handled securely by our payment gateway partner, Stripe Inc., in full accordance with Canadian privacy legislation (PIPEDA) and PCI-DSS compliance. GILC does not store raw financial card details on our servers.

4. Contact Officer

For inquiries regarding privacy or your data rights, please contact our Privacy Officer at gilcollaborative@gmail.com.
