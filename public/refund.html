Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Initial Consultation Assessment Deposits

Deposits paid for initial 1-on-1 academic advisory sessions (e.g. ¥500 CNY) are 100% refundable if cancellation is requested at least 48 hours prior to the scheduled consultation video conference.

2. Comprehensive Faculty Matching & Research Tracks

For custom research tracks and faculty mentorship slots, once a syllabus has been finalized and a university faculty mentor confirmed, deposit refunds are processed on a pro-rata basis subject to administrative onboarding fees as detailed in individual program agreements.

3. Refund Processing Times

Approved refunds are returned via the original payment method (WeChat Pay, Alipay, or credit card) within 5 to 10 business days in compliance with banking clearing standards.

4. Assistance

To request a refund or reschedule, email gilcollaborative@gmail.com with your payment confirmation number.
